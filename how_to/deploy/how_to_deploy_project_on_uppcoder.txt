=================================
How to deploy project on Uppcoder
=================================

`Uppcoder <https://portal.uppcoder.com/jampy>`_ runs Jam.py v7 in a container of its own, at its own HTTPS
address, with the Application Builder, a terminal and VS Code in the browser. Nothing is installed on your
computer.

Try it first, without an account
--------------------------------

* Open https://portal.uppcoder.com/jampy and start the **Music Store** demo or an **Empty project**.
  It runs in a sandbox of your own for two hours. Sign up at any point to keep what you made.

Deploy a project you keep
-------------------------

* Sign up at https://portal.uppcoder.com and click the link in the confirmation email. Then go to
  **Billing**, **Available Plans**, and start the free plan (**Spark**: one app, no card needed).

* Open **Stacks**, **Create**, **Blank stack**. Type "jam" in the search box and pick **Jam.py**.

* On the Jam.py card choose:

  * **Jam.py version**: 7.0
  * **Start from**: **Empty project**, or the **Music store demo** (customers, invoices, 3,504 tracks)

  Give the app a name. The project uses SQLite.

* Press **Deploy**, check the summary, then **Confirm and deploy**. The stack page shows the app's address
  when it is running.

* Open the address and add */builder.html* for the Application Builder. It signs in with your Uppcoder
  account, so there is no separate builder password to manage.

Working on the project
----------------------

* The stack page has a **terminal** (with the app server's log), a **file manager**, the project's **SQLite
  database** to look at its tables and data, and **VS Code** in the browser. The project lives in
  */home/uppcoder/app*.

* Use **Git** from the terminal or VS Code to keep the project in a repository of your own, for example on
  GitHub.

* To use your own domain instead of the Uppcoder address, a paid plan is needed. See
  https://portal.uppcoder.com/pricing
